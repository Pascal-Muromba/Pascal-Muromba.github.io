I am a PhD student in International Development at the University of Ottawa, in the School of International Development and Global Studies. I am supervised by Professor Joshua Ramisch and Professor Chris Huggins.

My doctoral research examines the adoption of digital and mobile technology among smallholder farmers in Zimbabwe. I am interested in how gender shapes access to and use of these technologies, and what adoption means for sustainable intensification. I also work on women's household decision-making and children's education in Zimbabwe, using Demographic and Health Survey data.

Before coming to Ottawa, I completed a Master of Agricultural Science at Tohoku University in Japan, in the International Development Studies Laboratory. I hold a bachelor's degree in innovation and economics from Ritsumeikan Asia Pacific University. I have also taught IB Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches HL at a secondary school in Sendai.

I grew up in Masvingo, Zimbabwe. I speak English, Japanese and Shona.
