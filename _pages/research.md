## Doctoral research

My doctoral project studies the adoption of digital and mobile technology among smallholder farmers in Zimbabwe. It looks at how gender shapes adoption and how mobile services relate to sustainable intensification on smallholder farms.

## Work in progress

- Gender and the adoption of mobile agricultural technology services among beneficiaries of Zimbabwe's Fast Track Land Reform Programme.
- Women's household decision-making and children's education in Zimbabwe, using Demographic and Health Survey data.
- Women's income autonomy, asset types and children's educational attainment in rural Zimbabwe.
