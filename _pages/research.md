## Research interests

Development studies, agrarian studies, and gender and agricultural development.

## Doctoral research

My doctoral project studies the adoption of digital and mobile technology among smallholder farmers in Zimbabwe. It looks at how gender shapes adoption and how mobile services relate to sustainable intensification on smallholder farms.

## Master's thesis

Gender and mobile agricultural technology adoption among Fast Track Land Reform Programme beneficiaries. Tohoku University, 2026.

## Fieldwork

In 2025 I coordinated primary data collection across 300 households in Zimbabwe's Fast Track Land Reform settlements for a study of post-COVID-19 child labour. I trained and supervised 10 enumerators and cleaned and validated the resulting dataset in R.
